Get Involved

Bring your experience. Share your ideas.

UAAT welcomes enquiries from individuals, businesses and organizations interested in exploring collaboration.

Ways to Participate

There is more than one way to contribute.

Start with a conversation about your goals, expertise and the area where you see a potential fit.

▣

Business Partners

Explore how your business might contribute to a shared initiative, service concept or community project.

✦

Sponsors & Contributors

Discuss sponsorship, in-kind contributions, financial support or other forms of participation.

◉

Media Organizations

Connect about broadcasting, interviews, coverage, content production and promotional relationships.

⌂

Community Organizations

Share ideas related to local engagement, skills, community development and economic opportunity.

✎

Students & Emerging Talent

Ask about internship and learning opportunities referenced in UAAT’s program materials.

⚙

Innovators & Creators

Introduce your ideas in arts, media, sports, technology, energy or the skilled trades.

Start a Conversation

Tell us what you would like to build together.

Share a little about yourself or your organization, the division that interests you and what kind of collaboration you have in mind. The UAAT team can then advise whether there is a relevant current opportunity.

  • Introduce your organization and experience.
  • Choose a division or program area.
  • Describe the contribution or partnership you have in mind.
