Who We Are

Creating real opportunities by bringing people together.

United Americans for the Arts and Trades (UAAT) is a nonprofit organization whose work and proposals span creative industries, skilled trades, technology, sports and community development.

The organization’s central idea is simple: meaningful progress takes collaboration. By connecting people, businesses, creative professionals and community partners, UAAT seeks to encourage new ideas and pathways to opportunity.

  • Encouraging creativity, entrepreneurship and practical skills.
  • Connecting contributors, sponsors, partners and communities.
  • Exploring ideas across arts, media, sports, energy and building.
