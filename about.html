About United Americans

Many perspectives. Shared opportunity.

Learn about the purpose, values and collaborative approach behind United Americans for the Arts and Trades.

Our Story

Bringing creative ideas and practical trades into one conversation.

United Americans for the Arts and Trades (UAAT) describes itself as a nonprofit organization focused on creating opportunities through collaboration across a range of industries.

Its published materials discuss projects and concepts in arts, entertainment, sports, media, technology, renewable energy, building materials, internships and economic development. The organization’s vision emphasizes bringing people and businesses together to explore ideas that may benefit communities.

This redesigned site organizes those themes into clear areas so visitors can understand the organization’s interests and make contact about potential collaboration.

Our Direction

Mission, vision and values.

These statements summarize the themes expressed across UAAT’s published materials in clear, current language.

◎

Our Mission

To encourage lasting opportunities by connecting people, businesses and communities through creativity, skills, innovation and collaboration.

✧

Our Vision

A future in which ideas, talent and shared effort help open new paths for individuals and communities.

♡

Our Values

Equality of opportunity, practical creativity, partnership, community contribution and a commitment to learning.

Our Approach

Ideas become stronger when people work together.

UAAT’s materials describe a cross-industry approach: creative projects can connect with media and promotion; sports and entertainment can create community engagement; and trades, technology and building concepts can open conversations about skills and manufacturing.

The organization welcomes conversations with businesses, educational communities, media organizations, sponsors and individuals interested in its areas of focus.
